The songs

Being in the public domain means the underlying text is free to use; it does not make our particular presentation of it yours to redistribute, and it does not extend to whatever you may separately need to project, print or record music in your own setting. Small group sessions

Sessions carried over the internet pass through a relay we operate. It forwards the current verse and nothing more, and it holds no history — but it is not end to end encrypted, and anyone who has a live join code can follow along. Treat a session as "not secret". The Privacy Policy explains exactly what passes through.
